# PATCHWORK WORLD

A dark woven environment for a world accumulated through play. The base is #211E16, reading cloth #3E3727, recessed cloth #2C291E, text #F0E0BD, quiet text #D3C3A0 and working thread #E5AA75. Literary serif titles and portable sans controls distinguish narrative reading from manipulation. Stitched edges, woven bitmap material, asymmetric cue pieces beside a broad scene and a shared preparation horizon give each kind of material a physical role. The raster recedes behind dark reading surfaces. No ambient motion is required; reduced motion removes transitions.

Tonight, World & relationships, comparison, capture and native editing retain the same campaign ledger and explicit provenance. A seam never creates a relationship, player consent or canon approval. The dark default remains Night Routes; Patchwork World offers tactile preparation and broad relationship reading.
