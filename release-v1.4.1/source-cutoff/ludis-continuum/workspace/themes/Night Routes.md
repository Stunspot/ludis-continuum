# NIGHT ROUTES

🎨 NAME: Night Routes

🌈 COLOR PALETTE:
Base: #0D1716 — near-black green mineral darkness
Primary: #182B25 — smoky glass working surface
Highlight: #F5AC70 — copper-amber directional light and action
Accent: #B7D0AE — dim reflected sage for secondary bearings
Text: #E9E8D8 — warm ivory, legible under low light

🧠 MOOD: Quiet urgency with spatial confidence; the GM can see the next junction without pretending to know which way players will turn.
❤️ EMOTION: Attentive anticipation, agency under pressure, trust in the record.
🔤 FONT PREFERENCE: Narrative reading in a warm literary serif; route numbers, sources, status and controls in a compact clear sans. System fallbacks keep the package portable.
🔍 VISUAL MOTIF: Dark mineral depth crossed by sparse copper routes; light collects at the current focus and selected waypoint, while recorded links appear as explicit bearings.
💬 VIBE TAGS: after-hours navigation, mineral glass, copper route light, visible choices, governed memory.
🌀 ANIMATED EFFECTS: No ambient motion. Selection shifts local light and edge emphasis; reduced-motion users see the same state instantly.
📦 USE CASE: Default dark Campaign Table working environment for live play and world reading.

The layout places the current situation in a wide smoked-glass field, near horizon and prep at its edge, and ordered cues below as waypoints. The World view reads source and relationships without inventing graph links. Texture lives behind reading planes; the surface uses layered radial light, inset reflection, backdrop blur and a generated mineral backdrop (`../night-routes.png`). Controls remain legible when the artwork is unavailable. Status, visibility, and authority use words and boundaries, not glow alone.

This is a newly authored Ludis theme, not a variant of the retired Theatre of choices. Implementation: `../skins.css`, `../index.html`, and `../app.js`. The saved raster is original generated material for this application. The Campaign Table does not infer plot, canon, or player approval from the visual routes.
