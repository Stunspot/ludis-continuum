# Description

Ludis Continuum helps players and GMs create, prepare and play tabletop adventures. Its creative skill shapes characters, worlds, scenes, encounters and consequences. Its gaming table keeps the practical work in reach: existing RPG folders, maps, handouts and documents; characters and resources; session openings and notes; real dice and encounter order. Choose the campaign and source locations in the app, bring useful material to Tonight, and resume the same work later. The offline table supports the conversational skill rather than pretending to generate fiction on its own.

# Usage Notes

Install the complete supplied Codex or Claude runtime, then ask “Open Ludis” or use its Windows/macOS launcher. Python 3.10+ is needed for the table. Start or open a campaign, link your RPG folders in Materials & maps, and bring a document or map to Tonight. Campaigns & location is always available for another folder or recovery. Document/image/audio previews, source unlinking, session recap downloads and campaign backup/restore are included. Original sources stay in place; imported copies belong to the campaign folder.

For creative help, ask Ludis naturally or copy a focused brief from Create & prepare into your AI conversation. The original32 creative instruments remain available. VTT exports and public/player sharing remain separate intentional actions; selecting Player workspace changes layout, not file permissions or access control.

Public GitHub Repo: [ludis-continuum](https://github.com/Stunspot/ludis-continuum)
