# Ludis Campaign Table · working environments

The Campaign Table keeps one governed native ledger. Each working environment changes only how the GM sees and navigates it. Theme preference stays in browser local storage; it never alters campaign records.

**Night Routes** is the dark default. A mineral backdrop, smoky glass, directional copper light and quiet sage reflection make the current situation feel like a junction. Ordered cues sit below it as waypoints; recorded links remain explicit. See [the theme record](<themes/Night Routes.md>) and `night-routes.png`.

**Patchwork World** is the dark woven counterpart. Lichen-dyed moss, flax and a small madder thread give the world the tactility of material gathered through play. Cues, scene and preparation have distinct physical roles; the woven image recedes behind reading surfaces. See [the theme record](<themes/Patchwork World.md>) and `patchwork-world.png`.

Both environments cover empty, populated, editing, comparison, capture, native-ledger and error states at desktop and narrow widths. Visual lines and seams never create facts. Proposal capture remains GM-only, active canon remains governed, and player reading remains an unapproved candidate.
