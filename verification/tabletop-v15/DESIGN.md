# Ludis 1.5.0 experience delta

Baseline: accepted1.4.1. Treatment: UPDATE to1.5.0, because source intake/location control and substantive session/player workflows expand the product promise. The user's authorized request makes everyday TTRPG usefulness the acceptance criterion.

A player or GM enters with an existing game, ideas, documents, images, or an empty beginning. The table earns its place by keeping useful material and decisions visible before and during play: open/create a campaign, connect the material that matters, prepare an opening, keep maps and character state close, resolve turns and dice, capture what happened, return to the same session. The native ledger is persistence, not the information architecture. An unconfigured folder, inaccessible source, failed save or unsupported preview must offer a visible next move.

Retained: existing native objects and explicit links, authored background art, original32 creative cores, native export tools and data history. Changed: the whole entry-to-session journey and normal editing surface. Ordinary content is read or copied without overwriting its originals. Creation uses domain-specific drafting guidance and an honest conversational handoff; the local app contains no model service and claims no generated fiction.

Checks: verification/tabletop-v15.cjs performs the normal first launch without a schema fixture, creates the campaign through the UI, reads ordinary linked Word/image/text material, imports a file, prepares playable material, tracks a character/encounter/roll/event, saves, resumes, exports a recap and recovers from missing paths. See result.json and screenshots for observed boundaries. A fresh independent review remains separate from this author-run result.
